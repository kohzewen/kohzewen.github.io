Outreach & Mentorship

As a scientist, I hope to build academic communities driven by the principles of open collaboration and inclusivity. I am particularly passionate about creating educational pathways into science and research mentorship. You can find a few highlights of my outreach work below:

Outreach

  • 2024 – present

    Application Mentorship Program

    I help run my department's Application Mentorship Program, which provides guidance to students applying to graduate school. The goal is to address gaps for young scientists who lack access to academic resources, e.g., at non-R1 research universities. We match over 50 students per year with graduate student mentors.

  • 2024 – present

    Letters to a Pre-Scientist

    I volunteer as a scientist pen pal through the Letters to a Pre-Scientist program. This program is a pen pal initiative that connects fifth to twelfth grade students from low-income schools across the country with STEM professionals.

  • 2025 – present

    Let's Invest in K-12

    I volunteer through MIT’s Let’s Invest in K-12 (LINK-12) program, which fosters connections between scientists and K-12 schools in the Boston area. As a classroom volunteer, I share stories from my personal journey to becoming a researcher, help students explore different career paths in STEM, and answer questions about science.
